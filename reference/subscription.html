Subscription & Usage API

Plans, usage, quotas, overages and app-owner billing across WEB4 to WEB10.

Plans and Checkout

GET/api/subscription/plans
POST/api/subscription/checkout/session
GET/api/subscription/checkout/session/{sessionId}
POST/api/subscription/billing/portal

List plans, start a Stripe checkout, check its result, and open the billing portal to change or cancel a plan.

Your Usage

GET/api/subscription/usage/current
GET/api/subscription/usage/series?from={date}&to={date}
GET/api/subscription/usage/estimate
GET/api/subscription/usage/events?limit=100
GET/api/subscription/usage/audit?limit=100

Current quotas, usage over time, a pay-as-you-go estimate (so far and projected), individual operations and the immutable audit trail. You only ever see your own usage.

Overages

GET/api/subscription/overages/prices
GET/api/subscription/overages/statements
POST/api/subscription/overages/pay-now

Usage beyond your plan needs your consent first; it is billed monthly, or you can pay a statement straight away.

Metering for Services

POST/api/subscription/usage/authorize
POST/api/subscription/usage/start
POST/api/subscription/usage/settle

WEB5 to WEB10 reserve, start and settle each metered call against the caller's subscription. Replaying the same operation returns the original decision, so retries never double-charge.

App Owners and Admins

App owners see usage for their own apps under /api/subscription/app-owner/*, including pay-as-you-go with a monthly cap. Administrators use /api/subscription/admin/*. The full list of routes is in the WEB4 Postman collections.
