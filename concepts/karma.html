OASIS Docs/Karma System
Concept

Karma System

Karma is OASIS's cross-platform reputation layer. It accumulates across all OApps and can be used to gate features, unlock content, and surface trustworthy contributors.

Design Principles

  • Cross-OApp — karma earned in one OApp is visible in all others. A player's reputation travels with their avatar.
  • Transparent — the full karma history (Akashic Record) is accessible via API. Every award and deduction has a source and reason.
  • Positive-sum — the system is designed to encourage cooperation, creativity, and helping others — not zero-sum competition.
  • OApp-sovereign — OApps define their own award events and amounts. OASIS provides the infrastructure; OApps define the economy.

The Akashic Record

Every karma event is recorded in the avatar's Akashic Record — an immutable, append-only log of every positive and negative karma event, including source OApp, event type, timestamp, and amount. This makes karma auditable and resistant to retroactive manipulation.

OApp Integration Pattern

A good OApp karma integration follows these rules:

  1. Award on meaningful events only — quest completion, helping another player, creative contribution. Not for clicking buttons or logging in daily.
  2. Use specific karma types — CompletedQuest rather than JoinedOAPP for quest events. Specific types make the Akashic Record useful.
  3. Gate content on karma thresholds — use GetAvatarsWithKarmaGreaterThan to unlock hard content for high-karma players.
  4. Deduct sparingly — negative karma should reflect genuine antisocial behaviour, not minor mistakes.
